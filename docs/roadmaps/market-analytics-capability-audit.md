## Purpose and method

This audit establishes the Noesis repository baseline and bounds its first market-analytics release. It is based on code, contracts, documentation, and completed implementation issues present in the repository on the date above. Two public macro connector paths were called against live Eurostat and World Bank endpoints; the configured FRED and SEC paths could not be reached because their credentials are absent. The in-memory quantitative calculation was exercised. No production deployment, persistent warehouse, market price feed, or analyst workflow was exercised.

“Implemented” below means a reusable capability exists in the repository. “Partial” means it exists but does not yet meet the market use case. “Unverified” means source code/configuration indicates a path, but readiness against a live provider has not been checked. Closed predecessor issues show implementation history; they do not establish current production readiness.

### Source and availability semantics

The SEC describes company submissions and extracted XBRL facts as JSON REST APIs at `data.sec.gov`; this supports the existing connector, but does not by itself supply equity prices or every normalized metric. [SEC developer resources](https://www.sec.gov/about/developer-resources) · [EDGAR APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces)

FRED exposes current FRED values and historical ALFRED real-time periods through `realtime_start`/`realtime_end`; the Noesis adapter now accepts an explicit `vintage_date` and records local acquisition separately. The release-date endpoint returns source-published calendar dates, which the provider says do not necessarily match data availability on FRED or ALFRED. Noesis's economic release snapshot selects only vintages already retained locally and does not infer missing historical values from today's data. [FRED real-time periods](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/realtime_period.html) · [FRED release dates](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/release_dates.html) · [ALFRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/alfred.html)

The ECB Data Portal offers both data retrieval and metadata discovery over SDMX. Noesis has an SDMX adapter supporting ECB, Eurostat and Bundesbank provider configurations, but the audit did not verify live coverage, licenses, or each configuration. [ECB SDMX services](https://data.ecb.europa.eu/help/getting-data-web-services-sdmx-0)

## Initial product scope

### Primary user and job

**Primary user:** a research analyst comparing public companies and explaining the evidence behind an industry view.  
**Primary job:** “Compare these companies, explain how their financial and market performance changed, show what evidence supports or challenges the explanation, and let me reproduce the analysis using information available at a chosen date.”

Portfolio monitoring and macro research are adjacent users/jobs. The first release is for research and analysis; its scope does not include placing trades or recommending trades to an individual.

### Beta boundary

- U.S.-listed common equities, represented as separate issuers, securities and exchange listings.
- Daily end-of-day prices and volume, with a target of the next available provider refresh after the close; no intraday or exchange-real-time promise.
- Company filings and normalized fundamentals; begin with the five mapped SEC facts already present, then expand financial-statement coverage as separately accepted work.
- U.S. and selected global macroeconomic series with explicit release/vintage availability; use existing economic providers and release snapshots.
- Company overview, user-selected peer comparison, time-series views, basic company filters, source watches and a cited research brief.
- Use Microsoft (`MSFT`), Oracle (`ORCL`), Salesforce (`CRM`), Adobe (`ADBE`) and ServiceNow (`NOW`) as the provisional five-company validation universe for U.S.-listed enterprise software. Validate and cite the industry membership before treating this sample as a market taxonomy or publishing a comparative research result.
- A licensed data provider must be selected and its rights/coverage confirmed in #1655/#1664 before external market-price coverage is claimed.
- Start with the available history from the selected source and display coverage gaps. A ten-year daily-history target is a provider-evaluation criterion, not a promise until coverage and rights are confirmed.

### Explicit first-release exclusions

Intraday feeds, execution/brokerage, options and other derivatives, fixed income, commodities, digital assets, professional consensus estimates, licensed analyst research, full ownership/holder history, automatic investment advice, and universally complete international issuer coverage remain externally gated. Provider-neutral fixture engines now cover fixed-income math, FX/commodity quote and roll semantics, European option analytics, digital identity/supply/reorg receipts, international coverage matrices, and intraday replay; these do not claim live sources, rights, capacity, or analyst acceptance.

### Provisional acceptance measures

Confirm thresholds after provider evaluation and phase-0 estimation. The beta must at minimum:

1. Reconcile each displayed calculation against a documented independent reference case, with formula, units, inputs and source revisions inspectable.
2. Reopen a saved analysis at a historical cutoff without substituting later filings, prices, macro revisions or documents; explicitly identify any missing historical input.
3. Show the source and freshness of company, price and macro values and suppress or label results when required inputs are missing or stale.
4. Compare five companies in a declared industry and export a cited brief including assumptions, uncertainty, conflicting evidence and coverage limitations.
5. Distinguish deterministic fixture/contract evidence from live-provider validation and analyst acceptance.

## Risks and decisions

| Risk / decision | Impact | Mitigation / owner |
|---|---|---|
| Price and corporate-action licensing | A feed may permit internal analysis but prohibit user display, retention, derived-data redistribution or exports. | Finish #1655 and #1664 before enabling a provider; security/data-rights owner records each permission and tests it across API, UI, caches and exports. |
| Historical data look-ahead | Revised filings, economic data, corrected prices and modern constituent lists can make historical analyses unknowable or biased. | Store source revision, public/release time and local acquisition time separately; require point-in-time snapshots, delisting coverage and explicit missing-history status. |
| Identity and corporate actions | Tickers change and can be reused; mergers and dividends distort naive price comparisons. | Build stable issuer/security/listing IDs and dated alias history, preserve raw prices, and version adjustment rules before analytics depend on returns. |
| Macro coverage mistaken for market coverage | Existing FRED and statistical connectors may make the repository look finance-ready despite no quote feed or stock model. | Use this capability matrix and keep “market-ready” claims gated by #1671 and #1679. |
| Vendor or credential readiness | FRED requires an API key; EDGAR requires a configured descriptive user agent; commercial feeds may require purchase and exchange agreements. | Record `implemented`, `credential-blocked`, `live-verified`, and `unavailable` separately for every source; do not include secrets in evidence. |
| Financial statement comparability | Fiscal years, XBRL contexts, currencies, taxonomies, restatements and non-GAAP measures differ. | Preserve filing context and taxonomy, normalize with versioned mapping, reconcile to source filings, and expose unmapped concepts. |
| Scope growth | “Full market analytics” spans asset classes with different market structures and data rights. | Gate phase 5 separately per asset class; do not block or overstate the U.S. equity beta based on unbuilt asset classes. |
| User value remains unproven | Many tools and dashboards may not produce a useful research outcome. | Keep the five-company cited historical comparison as the first release gate and obtain analyst review in #1679. |

**Open decisions:** select the initial live-validation universe; choose end-of-day history coverage target after provider samples; determine whether FRED vintages should be retrieved directly via ALFRED or only from current local snapshots; set numeric freshness/coverage SLOs after provider and deployment measurements; assign named phase owners and confirm staffing.
